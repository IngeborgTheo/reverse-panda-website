Reverse Panda started from a simple idea: your phone's home screen is one of the most personal spaces in your life — so why should it look like everyone else's?

Maybe that's a question I've been asking for longer than I realized. Growing up with vitiligo taught me that standing out isn't something to hide. The things that make us different are often the things that make us memorable.

My obsession with customization started years ago, tweaking themes and icons on a jailbroken iPod Touch through Cydia when I probably should have been doing something more productive. Ever since then, I've been fascinated by turning technology into something personal.

Reverse Panda is built for people who don't fit the defaults. No tracking. No dark patterns. Just a launcher that lets you make your phone feel like yours.
